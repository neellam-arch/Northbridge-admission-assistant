# Northbridge College London — Courses 2027

## Level 3 Diploma in IT
A one-year course covering programming basics, networking, databases and digital skills.
Start dates: September and January. Study mode: full-time, 3 days a week on campus.
Progression: students who pass can apply to the HND in Computing.

## HND in Computing
A two-year Higher National Diploma covering software development, data, cyber security and AI fundamentals.
Start dates: September only. Study mode: full-time, 2 days a week on campus plus online learning.
Progression: graduates can top up to a full BSc at a partner university.

## Foundation Year in Business
A one-year course preparing students for a business degree, covering management, marketing, finance and study skills.
Start dates: September and February. Study mode: full-time, 3 days a week on campus.

## Python for Beginners (short course)
A 6-week evening course, one evening a week, for complete beginners. Online only.
Start dates: every month.
