# Entry Requirements

## Level 3 Diploma in IT
- GCSE English and Maths at grade 4 (C) or above, or equivalent.
- Minimum age 16.
- International students need IELTS 5.0 overall, or equivalent.

## HND in Computing
- 64 UCAS points (for example, two A-levels at grade C), or a Level 3 Diploma in IT pass.
- GCSE English and Maths at grade 4 (C) or above.
- Minimum age 18.
- International students need IELTS 6.0 overall, with no band below 5.5.
- Mature applicants (21+) without formal qualifications may be considered after an interview.

## Foundation Year in Business
- 48 UCAS points, or relevant work experience of 2+ years.
- Minimum age 18.
- International students need IELTS 5.5 overall.

## Python for Beginners
- No formal entry requirements. Students need their own laptop.
