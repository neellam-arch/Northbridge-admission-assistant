# Fees, Deposits and Refunds

## Tuition fees per year
- Level 3 Diploma in IT: £3,500 (home), £9,000 (international)
- HND in Computing: £6,000 (home), £11,500 (international)
- Foundation Year in Business: £5,500 (home), £10,500 (international)
- Python for Beginners: £180 for the full course

## Deposits
International students must pay a £2,000 deposit before a CAS can be issued.
Home students do not pay a deposit.

## Payment plans
Fees can be paid in 3 instalments. Payment plans must be agreed with the Finance Office.

## Refund policy
- Within 14 days of accepting an offer: full refund of any payments.
- After 14 days but before the course starts: refund minus a £250 administration fee.
- After the course starts: no refund, except in exceptional circumstances approved by the Head of Admissions.
- Visa refusals: international deposits are refunded in full on proof of refusal.
All refund decisions are made by the Finance Office, not by admissions staff.
