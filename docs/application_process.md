# How to Apply

## Steps
1. Complete the online application form on the college website.
2. Upload copies of qualification certificates and photo ID.
3. International students also upload their passport and English test results.
4. Admissions reviews applications within 10 working days.
5. Some courses require a short interview (HND mature applicants, Foundation Year applicants with work experience instead of UCAS points).
6. Successful applicants receive an offer by email.

## Checking application status
Applicants can check their status by logging in to the applicant portal. Admissions staff can confirm status by email once identity is verified.

## Offers
Only the Head of Admissions can issue, change or withdraw an offer.

## Complaints
Complaints are handled under the college complaints procedure. They must be acknowledged within 2 working days and passed to the Student Services Manager.
